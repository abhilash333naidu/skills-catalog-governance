# Skill consolidation junctions (2026-08)

**Type:** policy note
**Date:** 2026-08
**Session source:** mapping of where skill consolidation legitimately merges content vs
where it must only archive (the dedup/consolidation junction).

## The junctions

- **Junction 1 — pure dedup:** two skills are functionally identical clones. Action:
  pick survivor, archive the other. No content merge → no loss-check required (manifest +
  archive log only).
- **Junction 2 — consolidation (partial overlap):** skills overlap but each carries unique
  content. Action: survivor + merge of unique content → FULL Council Per-Group pipeline +
  loss-check + G1b post-merge parity.
- **Junction 3 — separation (do NOT merge):** skills share a name or folder but serve
  distinct functions (e.g. `coder` and `ceo` flavors). Action: leave separate; merging here
  is a category error. Group only for per-group survivor decisions, never content.

## Lesson distilled

- The mistake class is treating every duplicate-looking group as a merge. Merge only where
  unique content exists (Junction 2); otherwise archive (Junction 1) or keep separate
  (Junction 3).
